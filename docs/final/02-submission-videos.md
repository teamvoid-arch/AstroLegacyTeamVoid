# Submission Videos

Two scripts. The 240-second video is for local judging (working demo + NASA data sets named on camera, due 13 Nov, 6:30 PM). The 30-second video is for global judging (due 14 Nov, 12:00 PM, on YouTube or Vimeo). Both: English subtitles, team name and every member's name on screen, no face, voice or name of anyone under 18.

**Look of both videos:** real screen recordings of the app on a phone (1080 × 1920 capture, placed in a clean phone frame on the app's night-blue background), cut with full-screen moments from the app itself. No stock footage. Narrator: one adult team member (or the VIMO voice for VIMO's lines only), calm and warm. Music: the app's own theme. Every claim on screen has its NASA source in the corner for one second.
