## The Moon

### Ranger 7 (1964) — main story

- **Status:** Used once. Mission complete (impact 31 July 1964).
- **What it found:** Sent more than 4,000 photos in its last 17 minutes: the closest Moon pictures anyone had seen.
- **Note:** NASA History gives 4,308 images; an older NASA page gives 4,316, so we say "more than 4,000".
- ✓ [NASA History: 60 years ago, Ranger 7 photographs the Moon](https://www.nasa.gov/history/60-years-ago-ranger-7-photographs-the-moon/)
- ✓ [NASA NSSDCA Master Catalog: Ranger 7](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1964-041A)
- 📷 Photo: [First Image of the Moon taken by a U.S. Spacecraft (PIA02975)](https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA02975)

### Surveyor 1 (1966) — main story

- **Status:** Went quiet in 1967. Still standing.
- **What it found:** America's first soft landing on the Moon: the ground was strong enough to land on.
- **Note:** To be fair: the Soviet Luna 9 made the first soft Moon landing, in February 1966.
- ✓ [NASA NSSDCA Master Catalog: Surveyor 1](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1966-045A)
- 📷 Photo: [Surveyor 1 -- America First Soft Lunar Landing (PIA12910)](https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA12910)
